- `zhejiang-wanli.png`: Zhejiang Wanli University, [official school identity page](https://www.zwu.edu.cn/xxwxhwxg/list.htm). [Original PNG](https://www.zwu.edu.cn/_upload/article/images/4d/f1/62888d59441285d7020ad710129a/fcad0679-c5be-4247-9cad-35bcdc3282aa.png).
- `hangzhou-normal.png`: Hangzhou Normal University, [official school identity page](https://www.hznu.edu.cn/xxgk/xmxhxg/). [Original JPEG](https://www.hznu.edu.cn/upload/resources/image/2020/06/11/LFWKKRBXLE3DOPPFEQ5XOSBZKA5XO_500x500.jpg), re-encoded as PNG without changing its contents.

# ACG cover sources

Retrieved on 2026-10-02 from the corresponding Bangumi subject entries via the public subject API (`https://api.bgm.tv/v0/subjects/{id}`). Original cover artwork is credited to its respective creators and publishers; it is used here to identify the works in this personal portfolio. The page preserves the artwork's colors and proportions.

The four anime are named in [Wh0rigin's introduction](https://bgm.tv/user/wh0rigin). The three manga represent the three manga authors named in the introduction. H2 and Goodnight Punpun were selected by the user; Goodbye, Eri was selected from the profile's public reading collection. Their inclusion does not introduce a new claim that they are the user's favorite manga.
